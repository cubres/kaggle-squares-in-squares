We do not claim those constructions as our discoveries or grant a blanket license over third-party material.

The earlier portfolio also used credited public numerical layouts from `avazaku/squarecraft-beyond-the-grid-22-7417-lb` V1 and `lightningv08/22-6034-cuda-sweep-credited-packing-seeds` V2. Original source terms and notices continue to apply. These coordinate archives, competitor prose, and competitor programs are not included here.

The official metric is supplied separately from Kaggle. This repository records its hash and calls it only after the reader supplies the pinned file. It does not relicense the metric.

The generated opening artwork is linked through our public dataset. It is decorative and does not establish geometric validity. The numerical solver and exact-decimal interval checks provide that evidence.

Public reports are compact derivatives of retained experiment records. They omit local machine paths, credentials, session identifiers, browser state, and unrelated projects. Their hashes differ from the original internal receipts; `artifact-hashes.json` identifies the original evidence bytes separately.
